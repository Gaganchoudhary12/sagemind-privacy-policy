# Privacy Policy for SageMind AI

Last updated: March 2026

SageMind AI is designed to protect user privacy.

## Data Collection
SageMind AI does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to external servers.

## On-Device Processing
All AI processing happens locally on the user's device. Conversations and documents remain on the device and are not sent to any external service.

## File Access
If the user chooses to open documents (such as PDF files), the files are processed locally only for answering questions within the app.

## Third-Party Services
SageMind AI does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

## Data Security
All chat history and documents remain stored locally on the user's device.
